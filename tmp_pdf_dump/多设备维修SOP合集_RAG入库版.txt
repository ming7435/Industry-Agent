3.14.2 TC820LTYsi Installation Checklist
Installer – Use this checklist to assure a complete setup on the TC820LTYsi.
Installation Checklist
ITEMS TO BE COMPLETED BY QUALIFIED MACHINE RIGGER, IF NOT COMPLETED
FOLLOW THE SUBSEQUENT STEPS:
The Turning Center is packed on one wooden pallet during transportation:
• When arriving at the machine the rigger should have carefully disassembled the wooden pallet and removed all the loose parts
from the machine.
• The leveling screws on the machine should be thread down by the rigger approximately 1.5” as measured from the bottom of
the leveling screw to the bottom of the base casting.
• The rigger should have placed the machine on the 4 outside leveling pads.
• The coolant tank is packed separately on the pallet; the rigger should first remove any brackets holding the coolant tank in
place, then remove the coolant tank from the pallet and set it aside.
• The chip conveyor is packed separately on the pallet; the rigger should first remove any brackets holding the chip conveyor in
place, then remove the chip conveyor from the pallet and install the casters on swarf conveyer before placing it in the machine
casting.
Refer to section 3.3 Uncrating the TCsi, and 3.4 Lifting and Placing the TCsi in the
Service Manual.
The machine should be lifted and moved by a fork-lift truck
• Use a fork-lift truck with a minimum loading capacity of 15,000 lbs to lift and move the machine.
• Place the forks of the fork-lift under the machine.
• Make sure the forks of the fork-lift protrude past the far edge of the machine base and ensure the machine is balanced before
starting to lift and move it.
• Pay special attention to the machine balance while lifting and moving, please note that the weight of the machine is greater on
the headstock side.
• Do not stop the lifting motions suddenly during the process and prevent sudden movement as the machine could become
unbalanced.
• Do not raise the machine too high as this may create a loss of stability and cause the fork-lift truck to overturn.
• Have another person to help guide the way while lifting and moving the machine. Ensure nobody is in the way and the path is
not blocked before moving the machine.
• When placing the machine on leveling pads be careful when removing machine from the forklift truck, the machine is subject
to tipping over if placed even a little off balance.

3.7 Electrical Connection
The TCsi machines come either with 208VAC input or 480VAC input. A 208VAC to 480VAC transformer is required for a 208VAC
input. A 480VAC input does not require the transformer option.
DANGER!!! Only a qualified electrician should wire the electricity for your machine installation.
3.7.1 Main Power to the Machine Connections
The TCsi machine should be installed under the correct electrical environments. Before connecting the power wires, make sure
the voltage is the same for both the machine and the plant power.
Ensure the electrical cables have the same or better power rating as prescribed. Do not connect any power cable that might
cause an abrupt voltage drop or generate signal noises on the power panel of the machine. Seal the electrical cabinet from
moisture at the incoming power cable opening.

3.8 Machine Leveling
3.8.1 Leveling Procedure
The leveling of the TCsi machine consists of adjusting leveling screws (F1 – F4 and R1 – R4), shown in figure 2.8.1a, such that the
machine is level using either analog or digital instruments. If the machine is not leveled properly, it may become twisted. Even a
slight amount of twist will move centers out of alignment, and result in inaccurate work.

3.9 Placing the Coolant System
The TC820si machine is fitted with a 33-gallon coolant tank and pump that delivers coolant through the turret at 30psi. The
TC820LTYsi machine is fitted with a 50-gallon coolant tank and pump that delivers coolant through the turret at 70psi. Once the
machine is leveled the coolant tank can be placed. For the TC820si machine, with the pump removed starting from the front of
the machine, push the coolant tank through the center of the machine’s base casting. The front face of the coolant tank should
sit flush with the sheet metal guarding. For the TC820LTYsi machine, the coolant tank can slide in the right-hand side of the
machine under the chip conveyor with the pumps located at the back of the machine.

The chuck foot switch will be delivered in a box along with the TCsi machine. Place the foot pedal in a convenient position for

under the electrical cabinet close to the rear door. The connector only attaches one way, and the location is labeled inside the

To test its operation, with the door open press the foot pedal and the chuck jaws should actuate.
Figure 2.11.1a Chuck Foot Switch Figure 2.11.1b Switch Connection
3.11.2 Setting Up Quill Foot Switch

7.3.1 Automatic Tailstock Has Lost its Position

7.2 Tailstock Alignment
7.2.1 Tailstock to Spindle Centerline Alignment
The machine should have a common centerline between the spindle, turret pocket at the X0 position, and the tailstock. After a
crash occurs the spindle to tailstock centerline should be checked and adjusted accordingly. The maximum permitted error is
0.002” (0.05mm). To Check the current spindle to turret pocket centerline alignment:
1. Clamp an indicator in the chuck and move the tailstock so the indicator feeler is measuring the pocket of the tailstock quill.
2. Make sure that the measuring surfaces are clean. Any debris or dirt will affect the measurements. 3. Sweep the pocket of the
tailstock quill and measure the error in the pocket. The error must not exceed 0.002” (0.05mm). 4. If this value is exceeded you
will need to adjust your tailstock centerline.
7.2.2 Tailstock to Z-Axis Parallelism
It is important when using the tailstock, it is aligned to the Z-Axis motion on the machine. To check the tailstock parallelism:
1. Install a MT4 taper alignment tool into the tailstock quill. 2. Attach a magnetic base to the turret body. 3. Set up your indicator
so that it is reading off the side of the alignment bar. Be sure to have your indicator set up in a way to reduce any cosine errors
present in the reading. 4. Make sure that the measuring surfaces are clean. Any debris or dirt will affect the measurements. 5.
Move the turret in the Z direction so your indicator runs across the top of the alignment bar. 6. Record the difference in position
on your indicator from the start to the end of travel along the alignment bar. 7. Repeat this process taking a reading from the
top of the alignment bar. 8. The error along the top and the side of the alignment bar should be less than 0.001”/4”
(0.025mm/100mm). 9. If the indicator shows an error of greater than this your tailstock needs realigning to make it move parallel
to the Z-Axis motion in the machine.
NOTE: if you do not have a MT4 alignment bar, place a precision ground bar between centers on the headstock and tailstock
measuring the error along the top and side of the precision ground bar, see figure 6.2.2. The measured error across the span
should be < 0.001”/4”.

6.0 Sauter 12 Station Turret
6.1 12 Station Hydraulic Turret
The TCsi series is fitted with a 12-station turret from Sauter. The TC820si uses and Bolt-on Turret face and the TC820LTYsi is
fitted with a BMT45 Turret face with a live tooling drive. The BMT45 turret for the TC820LTY is available in a half index option
providing 24 indexing positions. The turret is driven by an AC servo motor and utilizes a hydraulic clamping mechanism and
proximity switch.
As the tool exchange command is issued, turret will be rotated to the selected tool position according to NC or manual
commands. When the selected tool traces the target location, the AC servo motor will stop immediately. The hydraulic system
will then lock the tool magazine to prevent the tool magazine from further traveling. The proximity switch is used for checking
the magazine’s clamping.
The rotation direction could be either cw or ccw. The tool is selected based on the shortest path to minimize the tool selection
time.
6.2 Troubleshooting the 12 Station Turret
6.2.1 12 Station Turret Out of Sync with Control Display
Occasionally the turret position will become out-of-synch with the control display. Typically, this is when the green highlighted
entry of the Tool List as shown in figure 5.2.1a shows a different number than the tool position number engraved into the turret
body at the current cutting position.

6.2.2 Turret Parallelism to Axis Motion
It is essential that the turret is set up and aligned parallel to the axis motion of the machine in order to ensure proper accuracy.

6.2.2.1 Turret to X-Axis Parallelism To check the turret parallelism to the X-Axis motion:

5.1 Maintenance
5.1.1 How to Clean the Touchscreen & Machine Control Panel (MCP)
The pendant is designed for low maintenance operation. Nevertheless, you must regularly clean the pendant.
WARNING!!! 1. Turn power off when cleaning the pendant. 2. Do not clean the device using compressed air or steam jets
because they can damage it.
Clean only the front panel with a soft cloth moistened with water of mild cleaning agent. Spray the cleaning agent or water on
to a cleaning cloth. Do not spray anything directly on the front panel. Never use caustic solvents or abrasive cleaners.
Use dry cloth to clean the backside of the pendant.
5.1.2 How to Clean the Electrical Cabinet Heat Exchanger Air Filter
1. Lift tab at the bottom of the filter which lifts the filter out of the slot in front of the fan, see figure 4.1.2.

5.3 Mechanical Adjustments
5.3.1 Gib Adjustments
WARNING!!!
Ensure to adjust gibs frequently and properly. Otherwise, this might result in loss of machine accuracy.
The gibs are vital to the performance of your TCsi Machine. Gibs should be:
• flat
• free of twist
• free of burrs
• free of blockages in the oil passages and channels Defective or scarred gibs must be replaced.
Shimming of gibs will not yield acceptable results. It is good machining practice to avoid the use of shop air to clean the chips
off a machine. This risks blowing chips into the sliding way surfaces and compromising the performance of the machine.
Because of long-term friction between the bed and carriage, wear may occur. Proper gib adjustment is necessary after the
machine has been operated for a long time. Adjust the gib by turning the gib adjustment screw (2) using torque screwdriver see
figure 4.3.1. Set torque to 1.2Nm (0.9 ft lbs).

directly to the electrical cabinet and is controlled using a button on the MCP or through the use of M-Codes. As the pump of the

3.15.1 Instructions to remove the 3 Jaw Chuck:
1. Unclamp the chuck and press the E-Stop in center of travel. 2. Remove 2 of the 3 jaws from the chuck. 3. Using the remaining

ql80s2_sop_safe_service_entry  检修前安全隔离与复机前检查
触发条件
trigger
任何拆盖、调整、清洁、维修或进入运动机构区域前
前置条件
preconditions
· 由受过培训的人员执行；电气故障由合格电工处理
· 确认工作区域照明良好、地面无油污和障碍
作业步骤
steps.action / steps.safety
1. 停止棒料机和车床，结束自动循环。
安全：确认棒料和夹紧装置处于稳定状态。
2. 关闭棒料机主电源；维修涉及运动或气动机构时关闭气源并释放压力。
安全：不得在带电或带压状态下拆除防护盖。
3. 拆盖前确认棒料机已与车床脱开，并隔离可能的车床启动信号。
安全：防止跨设备误启动。
4. 完成维修后安装全部防护件，清理工具和异物，检查电气部件未接触水/油。
安全：未恢复安全防护不得试运行。
5. 按对应 SOP 进行低速/手动验证，再恢复自动循环。
安全：先验证安全联锁和急停，再验证送料。
安全要求
ppe_and_safety
· 关闭棒料机电源并断开电源插头
· 关闭并释放气源/确认无气动压力
· 拆盖前将棒料机与车床脱开
· 不得把手伸入运动中的棒料机
· 不得踩踏皮带

· 维修完成后必须恢复安全防护件

acceptance_criteria

ql80s2_sop_alignment_anchor  棒料机与车床对中及锚固
触发条件
trigger
首次安装、设备移动后、报警45持续出现或发现棒料在主轴内刮擦/卡滞
前置条件
preconditions
· 棒料机断电
· 无气动压力
· 车床稳定且已找平
· 必要时确认棒料机与主轴后端间距不超过 200 mm，超出时准备延长件
作业步骤
steps.action / steps.safety
1. 将棒料机尽量靠近车床后部放置，用水平仪检查 X/Y 方向；在直线皮带导轨和退让导轨处测量。
2. 确认中心顶升螺钉与底座齐平后，松开底座两侧共 12 个槽孔螺钉。
3. 使用中心千斤顶均匀升降，使送料推料器在主轴后端上下居中；拧紧 12 个螺钉并复查水平。
4. 让推料器分别在主轴后端和夹头后端居中，反复调整棒料机后部和前部，直到两个位置均对中。
5. 升起上料台，手动将直棒料推入主轴，确认前后均无卡滞、刮擦。
6. 用 4 个锚固螺栓固定设备；不得锚固在退让导轨中心位置。锚固后重新复查水平和对中。
7. 接通电源后执行上料台校准，再装载棒料进行最终送料验证。
安全要求
ppe_and_safety
· 遵循安全隔离 SOP

tools_and_materials

· 直棒料
验收标准

· 推料器在主轴后端和夹头后端均居中
